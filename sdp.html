Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common booking questions.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Contact us at least 24 hours before your visit date with your reference number.

Do I need to print my ticket?

No. Show the reference number and a photo ID at the entrance.

Are children charged the full price?

Children aged 5 to 12 pay half the adult price. Under 5 enter free.

How many tickets can I book at once?

Up to 10 tickets per booking. For larger groups, use the contact form.
